    Forensic diagnostics

      How it works

      1 · Classifier

      A Swinv2 vision transformer trained to separate camera images from generated ones scores the whole image. That score is the verdict.

      2 · Heatmap

      The image is cut into a 4×4 grid and every tile is scored by the same model, showing where the signal comes from.

      3 · Provenance

      We read C2PA Content Credentials, IPTC source type, EXIF and PNG generator tags. These are reported beside the score, never mixed into it.

      Read the result carefully

      • This is a probability, not proof. Don't use it as the only basis for accusing anyone or rejecting anything.
      • Why the bands are so wide. Scores cluster at the extremes and the model is poorly calibrated in the middle. In our own n=50 benchmark, 20% of real photos scored above 0.5, so we only say "likely AI" above 0.99.
      • Compression pushes scores down. WeChat, WhatsApp and similar apps recompress images, which lowers scores. Noise and grain push them up. Upload the original file when you can.
      • New generators can evade it. Recent models (Midjourney v7, FLUX dev, Firefly 4) are hard for every public detector. A low score does not clear an image.
      • Missing metadata means nothing. Most platforms strip it. Metadata that is present and names a generator is strong evidence, though.
